B.R.M Chronographes is a French watchmaking company specializing in mechanical chronographs inspired by automotive design. Their watches are machined in bulk and hand‑assembled in Magny‑en‑Vexin, bearing the EPV label for exceptional heritage. The brand offers a range of models with distinctive dial designs and high‑quality movements, catering to enthusiasts of precision timepieces and classic French craftsmanship.

**Areas:** Company · Watchmaking · Mechanical · French · Luxury
